Finite Angels and Devils!

This is ARandomTaiwaneseGuy(YT)/ RandTWGuy(Github)'s Finite version of the Angels and Devils game .
Here, we only consider Angels of power 1, which moves like a chess king. After the Angel chooses her starting square, the Devil blocks a square so the Angel can't move there in the future, and the Angel moves one step in any of the eight cardinal directions. The Devil only has a certain amount (the Arsenal) of blocks available. If the Devil is able to trap the Angel, he wins; if he uses up his arsenal, then the Angel wins instead. The Arsenal should be determined beforehand by consensus of both players.
